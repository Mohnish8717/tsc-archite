=== Customer Interview: Sarah Jenkins (Lead Product Manager, Enterprise Platforms) ===
Date: 2026-06-02
Interviewer: Lead PM, Simulation Engine
Notes:
Sarah expressed deep frustration with the current state of simulation validation. 
"When we present simulation outputs to our executive team to justify product decisions, they immediately question the validity of the agents. They look at the debate log and see names like 'CTO-Agent-1' or 'COMPLIANCE-DRIVEN-USER'. They tell me, 'This is just a pre-programmed script, not a real user.' It destroys our credibility in the boardroom.
We need the agents to use realistic human names like 'Marcus Vance' or 'Amina Diop' so the transcripts read like real advisory meetings. 
More importantly, we need to see *how* they arrived at their decisions. A plain text summary isn't enough. We need to visualize their psychological profiles. Having an interactive, neobrutalist three-tab modal that displays their MBTI cognitive style, OCEAN scales as visual progress bars, and their active stage in the buyer journey (Awareness, Trigger, Evaluation, Proof, Decision) would be a game-changer. It makes the system feel premium and provides the visual evidence our leaders demand."

=== Customer Interview: Arjun Venkatachalam (Senior Systems Architect, VBT Systems) ===
Date: 2026-06-03
Interviewer: Systems Architect Lead
Notes:
Arjun's concern is purely technical and telemetry-focused.
"The simulation is a black box. If an agent run takes longer than expected or deadlocks, I have no way of knowing what went wrong. I need real-time spawning telemetry. Show me the Agent ID, the Parent Simulation ID, the exact LLM model processing the turn (was it Gemini 2.5 Flash, or Gemma 4?), the cohort scale, and the active OS thread ID. 
Also, the current 'Catalyst for Change' model is a joke. It tells the agent 'you will change your mind if the PM gives a whitepaper.' That's not how human architects think. I change my mind if my core goals are met. If my goal is sub-millisecond precision, and a proposal helps me achieve that without adding bloat, I will adopt it. Otherwise, I will reject it. We need to give the LLMs goals and let them figure out the adoption logic autonomously.
Finally, we need a way to visualize the codebase. The 8-layer stack of the Predictive Reality Engine is highly complex. If we can integrate the 'graphifyy' package globally and query import paths or view dependency trees directly from our editor, it would save us hours of debugging."

=== Customer Interview: Sophia Patel (Lead Security & Compliance Auditor) ===
Date: 2026-06-04
Interviewer: Lead PM, Compliance
Notes:
Sophia focused on compliance and audit readouts.
"For our SOC2 Type II audit, we have to prove that our automated simulation tools do not introduce bias or run arbitrary code. Having boardroom debate transcripts with labels like 'Legal-Agent' or 'Security-Agent' makes the audit logs look robotic and artificial, which raises flags. Hardening the prompts to generate realistic, diverse human names is crucial for compliance.
We also need a clear audit ledger showing the exact sequence of cognitive turns. If the telemetry modal shows the active thread and parent run ID, we can cross-reference it with our central access logs to prove the simulation ran inside a secure sandbox."
